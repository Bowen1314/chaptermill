# HPR4748: Programmable Logic Controls - Episode 5

This episode explores the role of I/O modules in programmable logic controllers (PLCs), covering digital and analog input/output types, operator panels, and safety systems. It explains how PLCs interface with real-world devices and emphasizes that safety systems operate independently of the PLC. The episode also critiques claims about cybersecurity threats causing physical harm via PLCs, arguing such risks are overstated due to built-in safety controls. Listeners interested in industrial automation, control systems, or engineering will find this informative.
