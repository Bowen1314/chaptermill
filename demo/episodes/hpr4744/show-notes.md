# HPR4744: HPR Beer Garden 21 - Mild

Kevie, Paul, and Dave explore the history and characteristics of Mild ale, a once-dominant British beer style that has declined in popularity. They discuss its origins as young, unaged beer used in porter production, its evolution during the 20th century, regional naming differences across the UK, and its current niche status. Dave samples Banks's Mild, while Kevie tries a 1950s-inspired Burton Ruby Mild, sharing their tasting notes and reflections on the style's sessionability and flavor profile.
