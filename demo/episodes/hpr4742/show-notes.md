# HPR4742: OLF Call for presentations open

This episode covers the upcoming OLF (Open, Libra, Free) conference, returning to Ohio State University on November 20-21, 2026. Host Ken Fallon interviews conference chair Beth Lynn Eicher and speaker chair Vance Kogendurfer about the call for presentations, venue details, accessibility, and event structure. The show discusses presentation proposals, Birds of a Feather sessions, student involvement, and volunteer organization. It will interest open source enthusiasts, potential presenters, and those curious about community-run tech conferences.
