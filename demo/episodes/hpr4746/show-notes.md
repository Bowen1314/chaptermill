# HPR4746: Monotone

Lee investigates whether Hacker Public Radio episodes are 'monotone' by analyzing pitch variation and semantic content using Python tools. He compares HPR to other podcasts and TED Talks, finding that while speech pitch is flatter due to solo recordings, the range of topics is broad. The episode covers audio processing, sentiment analysis, and open-source libraries like Librosa and SciKit-learn.
