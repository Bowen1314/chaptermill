# HPR4740: Robert A. Heinlein in the 1960s

This episode explores Robert A. Heinlein's major works from the 1960s, a decade when he gained mainstream fame and openly expressed his views on politics, religion, and sexuality. Key novels discussed include Stranger in a Strange Land, Podkayne of Mars, Warfins of the Sky, Glory Road, Farnham's Freehold, and The Moon is a Harsh Mistress. The episode highlights themes of societal critique, libertarianism, and speculative fiction, noting awards and cultural impact.
