# HPR4745: BruCON interview 1: The BruCON organization

This episode features an interview with Tom Gillis and Kundurus from the BruCON hacker conference team, discussing the organization and culture of BruCON, a community-driven cybersecurity event in Belgium. Topics include the volunteer-based structure, the call for papers process, affordability for students, and the unique atmosphere that blends technical talks with hands-on activities like lockpicking and retro gaming. The hosts emphasize the importance of maintaining a community-focused, accessible event that fosters learning and connection. This episode will appeal to listeners interested in hacker culture, conference organizing, and grassroots tech communities.
